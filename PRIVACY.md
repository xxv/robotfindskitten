# Privacy Policy for robotfindskitten

*Last updated: September 23, 2026*

robotfindskitten is a little hobby project: a zen simulation where you, a
robot, wander around looking for kitten. I (Steve Pomeroy) made it for fun
back in 2009 and have kept it going ever since. It's free, it's open source,
and it has no interest whatsoever in you beyond helping robot find kitten.

## The short version

The app doesn't collect, store, or share any information about you. None.

## The slightly longer version

- **No data collection.** The app doesn't gather any personal information,
  usage statistics, device identifiers, location, or anything else.
- **No internet access.** The app doesn't request the Internet permission,
  so it couldn't phone home even if it wanted to (and it doesn't want to).
- **No ads, no analytics, no trackers.** There are no third-party libraries
  or SDKs in the app at all.
- **No accounts.** There's nothing to sign up for.
- **Nothing saved.** The app doesn't save anything about you or your games.
  When you close it, robot forgets everything. It's very zen that way.
- **No special permissions.** The app doesn't ask for any.

## Links in the About screen

The About screen has a few web links (to robotfindskitten.org and friends).
If you tap one, your web browser opens and you leave the app. From there,
those websites' own privacy policies apply.

## Google Play

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and
may collect information as part of running the Play Store. That's covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not this
one. As the developer, the Google Play Console shows me things like overall
install counts and anonymous crash reports (only if you've chosen to share
those with Google), but nothing that tells me who you are.

## Kids

The app is fine for all ages and doesn't collect anything from anyone,
children included.

## Changes

If this ever changes (it's hard to imagine why it would), I'll update this
page and the date at the top.
